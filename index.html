About

Idea to Play Store, one system at a time

I'm a Flutter developer from Minya, Egypt, studying Computer Science at Minia University. I build cross-platform mobile applications end to end — interface, state, backend, and deployment.

Most of my work centers on Flutter and Dart, structured with Clean Architecture and predictable state management through BLoC and Cubit, so a codebase stays legible as it grows past its first version.

On the backend I work with Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Messaging), Supabase, and custom Laravel / PHP APIs on MySQL — including JWT authentication and encrypted handling for apps that manage sensitive user data. I've taken apps through full deployment on the Google Play Store.

Below: two live education platforms built on a shared Laravel backend, a commerce platform currently in testing, and a set of open-source utilities.
