From a study app to a platform tutors run on

I'm a Flutter developer from Minya, Egypt, and a final-year student at the Faculty of Computers and Artificial Intelligence, Minia National University.

It started with my own faculty: study material was scattered across YouTube, WhatsApp, and Telegram, so I built an app for each year that puts it in one place. Together they passed 600 downloads.

Then I built a full learning platform for a real tutor. Other tutors asked for it, so I made it white-label — five Play Store listings from two codebases — and each tutor gets what their teaching needs: an equation editor for a math teacher, attendance and WhatsApp alerts to parents for another.

I also build websites with Technova, a startup in Minya and Beni Suef, including one for a client in the US.
