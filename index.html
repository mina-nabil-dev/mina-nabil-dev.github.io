About Me

Building Mobile Products with Flutter

I'm Mina Nabil, a Flutter developer from Minya, Egypt, and a Computer Science student at Minia University. I build cross-platform mobile applications that solve real problems and deliver a clear, reliable user experience.

My main focus is Flutter and Dart, with an emphasis on maintainable application structure, reusable components, and predictable state management using BLoC and Cubit. I also work with Clean Architecture to keep projects organized and easier to extend as they grow.

For backend functionality, I work with Firebase, including Firestore and Firebase Cloud Messaging, as well as Supabase and REST APIs. I've also handled application deployment and publishing through the Google Play Store.

My portfolio includes published student-focused applications, an actively developed commerce platform, and several open-source projects. I'm open to freelance work, remote collaboration, and interesting mobile app projects.
